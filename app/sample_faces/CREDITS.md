# Sample faces

AI-generated faces of people who do not exist, used as fake customers' ID photos and selfies in the demo data.
Each is cropped from a public-domain image on Wikimedia Commons (category "PD-algorithm").
`app/kyc_docs.py` turns them into specimen ID cards and selfies when the demo data is generated.
